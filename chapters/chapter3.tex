\chapter{Methodology}\label{cha: Methodology}

\section{Initial growth of a-Si and SiO\texorpdfstring{$_{2}$}{2} thin films via electron beam evaporation}\label{sec: growth}
Glass slides and Si wafers were used as substrates for the initial growth via electron beam evaporation of a-Si and SiO$_2$ thin films respectively. The substrates were cleaned by sonication in acetone, isopropanol, and deionized water for 5 minutes each, after which they were blow-dried with nitrogen gas. The substrates were then loaded into the electron beam evaporation chamber, where a base pressure of $\qty{4.5e-4}{\Pa}$ was achieved before actual deposition. Deposition was carried out at room temperature, and the nominal deposition rates and thicknesses of the films were monitored using a quartz crystal microbalance (QCM). After unloading the samples from the chamber, the thicknesses of the films were measured using x-ray reflectivity (XRR) and optical profilometry. The refractive indices of the films were measured using reflectance spectroscopy and Swanepoel's method with UV-Vis spectroscopy. The measured refractive indices of the a-Si and SiO$_2$ films were used for the design of the a-Si/SiO$_2$ ARC stack.

\section{Simulation and growth of a-Si/SiO\texorpdfstring{$_{2}$}{2} antireflective coatings}\label{sec: growth_and_simulation}
An implementation of the transfer matrix method in Python was used to calculate the optimal thicknesses of the Si and SiO$_2$ layers for the antireflective coating stack. The refractive indices of the a-Si and SiO$_2$ layers were obtained from the previous section. The optimal thicknesses of the layers were calculated to minimize the reflectance at $\qty{1550}{\nano\meter}$ and $\qty{780}{\nano\meter}$, which are the wavelengths of the pump and probe beams which can be used in the THz-TDS spectroscopy system. The program iterated over a range of thicknesses for each layer while calculating the reflectance of the stack, selecting the thicknesses that resulted in the lowest reflectance at the target wavelengths.
The initial thicknesses of the optimal ARC stack were obtained using the transfer matrix method with refractive index data from the films grown in the previous section.to obtain the thicknesses of the a-Si and SiO$_2$ films to be deposited. A Si wafer will be prepared for reactive RF magnetron sputtering by a standard RCA clean. The wafer will be mounted onto the substrate holder and then loaded into the sputtering chamber. A layer of SiO$_2$ will be deposited on the Si wafer by sputtering an SiO$_2$ target with O$_2$ as reactive gas, followed by a amorphous Si layer sputtered with a Si target. This process is repeated until two pairs of layers are deposited on the wafer. The thicknesses of the layers will be measured using scanning electron microscopy.

\section{Transfer of InGaAs photoconductive layer to a-Si/SiO\texorpdfstring{$_{2}$}{2} DBR}\label{sec: transfer}
The InGaAs thin film grown via MBE on the InP substrate will be transferred to the a-Si/SiO$_2$ DBR grown via reactive RF magnetron sputtering. The InGaAs film on InP will be coated with a thin layer of Apiezon wax for protection before the lapping of the InP substrate. After a sufficient thickness of InP is removed by lapping, the remaining InP substrate will be etched chemically by a HCl H$_2$O$_2$ solution, then the InGaAs film with Apiezon wax will be transferred to the a-Si/SiO$_2$ DBR by van der Waals bonding. A bowtie PCA pattern will be transferred to the sample via photolithography and metallized with AuGe/Ni/Au using resistive evaporation and electron beam deposition. The sample will undergo liftoff to remove excess metal and rapid thermal annealing.

\section{THz TDS of InGaAs on a-Si/SiO\texorpdfstring{$_{2}$}{2} DBR}\label{sec: tds}
The THz emission characteristics of the InGaAs on a-Si/SiO$_2$ DBR PCA will be measured with a standard THz-TDS spectroscopy system. The beam from a $\qty{1.55}{\micro\meter}$ femtosecond laser will be split into pump and probe beams using a beam splitter, with the pump beam exciting the InGaAs on a-Si/SiO$_2$ DBR PCA and the probe beam optically gating a THz detector. The time domain and representative frequency domain spectra of the InGaAs on a-Si/SiO$_2$ DBR PCA will be analyzed.
